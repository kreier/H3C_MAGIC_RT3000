Das U-Boot History, QSDK & uBootKit

How a 1999 PowerPC bootloader from Germany evolved into the universal embedded Linux bootloader Das U-Boot, why our RT3000 runs a 2016 Qualcomm QSDK fork rather than upstream mainline, and how uBootKit & IPQ50XX-Uboot build on top of it.

History of Das U-Boot (DENX, Tom Rini & Nabla)

Das U-Boot ("Universal Boot Loader", a playful German nod to Das Boot) is not a Linux tool written inside the kernel, but the de-facto standard standalone open-source bootloader for embedded Linux devices (routers, single-board computers, industrial controllers). Its history is closely tied to German embedded-Linux engineering:

1999–2012 · Origin & DENX

8xxROM → PPCBoot → Das U-Boot

Started as Magnus Damm's 8xxROM for PowerPC. Wolfgang Denk — founder of DENX Software Engineering (denx.de, Gröbenzell near Munich) — moved it to SourceForge in Oct 1999 as PPCBoot (SourceForge forbade leading digits). After merging ARMBoot, it was renamed Das U-Boot in Nov 2002. DENX hosted the official Git infrastructure (source.denx.de) and Wolfgang Denk led the project for over a decade.

2012–Present · Upstream

Tom Rini & Global Custodians

In late 2012, Tom Rini took over from Wolfgang Denk as upstream U-Boot's lead maintainer (head custodian), guiding modern multi-architecture development (ARM64, RISC-V, UEFI, CI) with a worldwide team of subsystem custodians at docs.u-boot.org / source.denx.de.

July 2025 · DENX → Nabla

Nabla Software Engineering

Following Wolfgang Denk's passing (2022), DENX's stakeholders announced voluntary liquidation in July 2025 to retire. Former DENX engineers formed Nabla Software Engineering (nabladev.com, Augsburg, Germany) to continue professional U-Boot, embedded Linux, BSP, and security consulting/maintenance.

Why the RT3000 Uses Qualcomm's Older u-boot-2016 Fork

While upstream mainline Das U-Boot is on modern 2025.xx / 2026.xx releases, almost all Qualcomm IPQ50xx/60xx/807x community bootloaders — including ours — are built from Qualcomm's u-boot-2016 branch (originally forked from upstream U-Boot 2016.01 for Qualcomm's QSDK 11.x / 12.5):

  • SoC-specific hardware drivers: Qualcomm added extensive out-of-tree drivers to u-boot-2016 for the IPQ50xx QPIC SPI-NAND controller + BCH ECC, ESS/QCA8337/RTL8367S Ethernet switches, and clock/GPIO trees that were never fully upstreamed into mainline U-Boot.
  • Seamless handoff with stock Stages 1 & 2: On the RT3000, we keep Qualcomm's factory 0:SBL1 and 0:QSEE (TrustZone) intact (see Boot Process & Stages). QSDK's u-boot-2016 speaks the exact SMEM, 0:MIBIB, 0:BOOTCONFIG, and TrustZone SCM ABI those earlier stages expect.
  • Proven bootipq dual-slot logic: Retaining QSDK's bootipq and BOOTCONFIG engine lets stock H3C firmware (Chaos Calmer / QSDK 4.4) and OpenWrt 21.02.7 (QSDK 11.5) coexist cleanly in Slot 1 and Slot 2.

From Qualcomm QSDK 12.5 to IPQ50XX-Uboot

Layer 1 · Qualcomm QSDK

qsdk/u-boot-2016 (NHSS.QSDK.12.5)

Base Qualcomm IPQ tree from CodeLinaro NHSS.QSDK.12.5 and 1980490718/u-boot-2016, combined with DHCP + web failsafe design inspired by Yuzhii0718/bl-mt798x-dhcpd.

Layer 2 · uBootKit

chenxin527/uboot-qsdk12.5-build

Direct upstream uBootKit for IPQ50xx / IPQ53xx / IPQ60xx / IPQ807x routers. Adds the Argon-style Web rescue UI, built-in DHCP server, and unified multi-SoC build tooling.

Layer 3 · IPQ50XX-Uboot

FlyFish-go/IPQ50XX-Uboot

Adds H3C NX30/RT3000/RC3000/RW3000 board support (HW-A–HW-D), runtime NAND & ECC auto-detection (v0.2.0+), vendor-logic BOOTCONFIG dual-slot switching, 80/116 MiB layout merging, pre-write checks, and BootBridge Web.

The full upstream commit history from chenxin527/uboot-qsdk12.5-build is preserved in FlyFish-go's earlier repository FlyFish-go/H3C-NX30-Uboot.

Where uBootKit Is Installed on Flash

U-Boot occupies two mirrored fail-safe slots (0:APPSBL and 0:APPSBL_1 — see 128 MiB Partition Layout and Mirror vs. Dual-Boot):

PartitionMTDAddress RangeSlot SizeuBootKit v0.2.0Stock U-Boot
0:APPSBL (Primary) mtd11 0x00500000–0x00640000 1,280 KiB (1.25 MiB) 628.2 KiB (49.1%) 415.6 KiB (32.5%)
0:APPSBL_1 (Mirror) mtd12 0x00640000–0x00780000 1,280 KiB (1.25 MiB) 628.2 KiB (49.1%) 415.6 KiB (32.5%)
  • Total allocated bootloader space: 2.50 MiB (1.95% of the 128 MiB NAND chip).
  • Actual binary footprint (h3c-hw-b.mbn, 643,280 bytes × 2): 1.23 MiB (0.96% of the 128 MiB chip).
  • Environment & slot selection: Runtime variables stay in 0:APPSBLENV (mtd10, 512 KiB); 0:SBL1 picks slot 0 or 1 via 0:BOOTCONFIG / 0:BOOTCONFIG1 (mtd2/mtd3).

Why v0.2.0+ Replaces Legacy v0.1.0

One Binary per Hardware Group
Legacy H3C-NX30-Uboot v0.1.0 shipped an 84-image matrix (4 board groups × 21 NAND/ECC combinations). IPQ50XX-Uboot v0.2.0+ replaces that with a single file per board group (h3c-hw-b.mbn for RT3000 HW-B) that probes the SPI NAND chip ID and ECC strength automatically at boot.
