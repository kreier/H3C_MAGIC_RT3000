Dual-Boot Switching & Future Upgrades

Switch between your stock H3C firmware (Slot 1) and OpenWrt 21.02.7 (Slot 2) using only the hardware RESET button—no PC required—and keep OpenWrt up to date safely.

Interactive LED Dual-Boot Timing (HW-B)

Hold RESET while plugging in power (on HW-B/HW-C use RESET; on HW-A/HW-D Mesh/WPS also works) and release based on the red LED flash count. Click any card to inspect the exact behavior:

Boots Slot 2 (rootfs_1 @ 0x03100000 — OpenWrt 21.02.7 / QSDK 11.5) and sets Slot 2 as default in 0:BOOTCONFIG.

40+40 MiB Dual-Boot vs. 80 / 116 MiB Merged Layouts

If you ever outgrow the 40 MiB Slot 2 overlay, you can switch to the 80 MiB or 116 MiB layout in the uBootKit Web Rescue page's partition tab before flashing a .ubi image (see the 128 MiB Partition Layout diagram to compare both). Keep in mind that merging erases the stock H3C system in Slot 1, though your old_firmware.7z backup and Web Rescue remain available for recovery.
